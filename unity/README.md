# Unity client

Unity project for the game client. Built with the built-in renderer and no extra packages so far.

- **Editor:** Unity 6.3 LTS, pinned to `6000.3.29f1`.
- **Settings:** `ProjectSettings/` and `Packages/packages-lock.json` are committed as generated by 6000.3.29f1, so opening the project should leave git clean.
- **Git LFS:** run `git lfs install` once per machine before adding art or audio. `unity/.gitattributes` sends images, models, audio, video and fonts to LFS.

## Blacksmithing minigame (first playable)

Open `Assets/Game/Scenes/Blacksmithing.unity` and press Play.

The first time you open it, an old smith explains each step once (the setup screen can replay it). After that the forge gives no help beyond a keys bar: no heat gauge, no markers, no timing zones, no quench timer. You read the colour of the metal, listen to the beat, and judge the blade against the ghost outline. The results screen explains what happened afterwards.

| Step | What you do | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Heat | Pull the bar when its colour looks right for the recipe. Too cold cracks it, too hot burns it. | SPACE (or click the forge) |
| Strike | A beat plays. On any beat, strike the part that needs work. Off the beat, or twice on one beat, dents the blade; overworking a part ruins its shape. | A tip, S body, D edge (or J K L, or click the part) |
| Done shaping | Set the hammer down when the blade matches the outline, or play out the beats. | E |
| Cooling | Cold metal stops moving. Reheat; reheats after the second cost quality. | R |
| Quench (Journeyman) | Pick water (hardest, risky), brine, or oil (safe), then hold for your own count. | 1 2 3, then hold SPACE |
| Finish | Three sharpening strokes; stroke the stone over the dull spot. Good strokes can add bonus traits. | SPACE |

Scoring follows the Crafting & Gathering Spec:

- Score `S = shape × strike accuracy × heat control`
- Quality `Q = floor + (ceiling − floor) × clamp(S ± 0.1, 0, 1)`, botch below 0.15 (Q 1–10)
- Ceiling = lowest of method, station, hammer, and the inputs' weighted quality (longsword: blade 60%, guard + pommel 25%, grip 15%)
- Skill widens the perfect window from ±60 ms (skill 1) to ±140 ms (skill 100) and raises the floor
- Better tools (hammer, station) raise the ceiling

Recipes are the iron path for level 1–20: Plain Iron Sword (Novice), Iron Shortsword (Apprentice), Iron Longsword (Journeyman).

## Mining minigame

Open `Assets/Game/Scenes/Mining.unity` and press Play.

A knowledge game: no timing, no aiming. The first time, an old miner explains the craft once (the setup screen can replay it). Nothing on screen says which block is ore or what to do next; the results screen explains what happened.

| Step | What you do | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | The face is a wall of blocks split by joints and bedding lines. The seam is one bed of it. Read the surface signs (streaks, stains, lichen, crystals) and tap blocks: ore rings, solid rock thuds, broken rock sounds hollow. Taps are limited (4 at skill 1, 8 at skill 100). | 1 Tap, then click / SPACE |
| Plan the cut | A block held on both sides and from above is locked. Prying locked ore shatters it, so open the face on barren or loose rock beside or above the ore first. A block with a free side shows a deep shadow along that edge. | |
| Tool per blow | Blocks come in two sizes: pick for small blocks, wedge for large ones, sledge breaks out rock anywhere but shatters ore. Wrong tool on rock wastes the swing; on ore it shatters it. 10 swings per node. | 2 Pick, 3 Wedge, 4 Sledge |
| Roof | Every block out loads the roof (more for the top bed and the sledge). Dust and creaks warn, earlier for skilled miners; tap the roof for solid / drummy. A prop (2 in the kit, costs a swing) eases it. Past the limit, each swing risks a fall that buries 40% of the cart and ends the dig. | Tap the roof strip, P prop, E stop |
| Sort | Keep or toss each piece by colour, lustre and heft. Stained rock mimics ore's colour; iron and copper ore are heavy for their size, coal is light. | click / SPACE, ENTER done |

Sign meanings are rolled per world (the setup screen can start a new world). The miner's journal records, for every sign, how often it sat on ore versus barren rock, and persists between digs.

Scoring:

- Yield = ore pieces kept (crushed count half), out of what the node held
- Quality = vein grade × (0.5 + 0.5 × whole) × (0.6 + 0.4 × purity); whole = share of ore taken out unbroken, purity = share of kept pieces that are ore
- Capped by the lower of the pick (worn 60, iron 85, steel 100) and skill (31 at skill 1, 100 at skill 100)
- Skill never adds swings; it sharpens the senses (more true signs noticed, more taps, earlier roof warnings)

## Code layout

```
Assets/Game/Crafting/Blacksmithing/
  Core/   rules only, no UnityEngine (asmdef with noEngineReferences) -> reusable on the C# server
  View/   ForgeView (placeholder visuals drawn with IMGUI), ForgeAudio (sounds generated in code)
Assets/Game/Crafting/Mining/
  Core/   rules only, no UnityEngine
  View/   MineView (IMGUI), MineAudio (sounds generated in code)
Assets/Game/Scenes/Blacksmithing.unity
Assets/Game/Scenes/Mining.unity
```

The Core rules are tested outside Unity: `dotnet test tests/Blacksmithing.Tests`, `dotnet test tests/Mining.Tests` and `dotnet test tests/Skinning.Tests` (needs the .NET 8 SDK).

## Skinning minigame

Open `Assets/Game/Scenes/Skinning.unity` and press Play. Hunting is ordinary combat; this is the skinning and hide work afterwards, turn-based with no timing or aiming: choose open or cased, feel/pull/cut each patch of hide free while the body cools, field-dress it before the meat spoils, clean the hide on the beam, then salt, stretch or roll it up untreated. Kept non-graphic: no blood, flesh or innards shown. Rules and numbers: `docs/skinning-minigame.md`.
